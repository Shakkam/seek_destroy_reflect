## 2026-07-30 — Scope clarified: two-tier MVP

- User confirmed a **two-tier scope structure**, not a single MVP:
  1. **Prototype / core-loop validation tier**: 1v1 local only, roster can be unbalanced — this is the true "is it fun" test.
  2. **Full MVP / marketable tier**: balanced roster + local AND online 1v1 + a small campaign — this is what needs to exist before considering the game "done enough to matter" as the user's actual bar.
- **No deadline / no time pressure** — explicitly a no-rush passion project ("m'en fous" re: timeline). This materially changes risk tolerance: netcode, balance iteration, and campaign content can all take as long as they need. Scope realism still matters for the brief's honesty, but urgency does not shape sequencing here.

## 2026-07-30 — Core fantasy / vision sentence

- User's own words: "Dans Seek & Destroy & Reflect the ball, le joueur ressent ce besoin d'occuper au mieux son espace, tout en ayant une vision 360 de ce qui se passe autour et un sentiment de puissance jouissive lorsqu'il bourrine son adversaire."
- This aligns with the brainstorming session's Player Fantasy Mining result (dominant: Positional Strategist; secondary: Power-Escalating Pilot; accent: Reactive Duelist) — confirms the brainstorming output rather than contradicting it.
- Open thread raised: whether "puissance jouissive" (bourriner) means power expressed through raw damage output/chaos, or through precision (a well-placed hit that hurts) — **resolved**: both are equally valid/satisfying (explicit Street Fighter 2 comparison — a rapid hurricane-kick-style flurry of small hits is just as satisfying as one heavy hammer-blow hit). This is not an either/or design choice; it reinforces the asymmetric roster direction — some characters lean toward rapid-fire/spam-feel weapons, others toward slow, heavy, high-impact single hits, and both should feel good.

## 2026-07-30 — Gameplay pillars confirmed

- Three pillars confirmed as-is: **Spatial Control**, **Return Precision** (aim + spin/lift), **Mastered Power Escalation** (fixed-kit roster + actively-filled gauges, not random loot).
- The candidate 4th pillar ("100% visual readability / no HUD") is confirmed NOT a gameplay pillar — it's a UI/art-direction constraint, to be documented separately in the brief, not in the pillars list.
- **Refinement to the no-HUD idea**: user overrides the Constraint Box's "zero HUD" framing (which was an exploratory exercise, not a locked decision) — an **HP bar/gauge is a must-have HUD element**, important for at-a-glance competitive readability of the opponent's state in 1v1. Weapon/gauge state can remain diegetic (wear/discoloration on the ship, weapon icon transparency + gold outline) as previously explored — only HP gets an explicit HUD treatment.

## 2026-07-30 — Target audience

- Audience framed around **temperament**, not just genre familiarity: players who enjoy competition, "bourrin"/stress-relief satisfaction, AND technical mastery/skill expression, together.
- Named reference points: Street Fighter players AND Brawl Stars players — explicitly NOT a gameplay-pacing reference (Brawl Stars remains a rejected pacing/feel model per the brainstorming session), but an **audience-profile** reference — confirmed by user: "attract that audience, but with a faster/more intense pace than Brawl Stars."
- Age: no upper bound specified; explicit lower bound sense of **12+** (user doesn't see appeal for under-12s).
- **Local vs. online as primary driver — genuinely undecided**, and treated as a legitimate open finding rather than forced into a single persona: user is ~50, personally shaped by couch competitive-fighting-game sessions (Street Fighter era); observes his own sons are drawn to quick online games (Brawl Stars-like). Two plausible audience segments coexist: (a) nostalgic couch/competitive fighting-game players, (b) younger online-first, accessible/fast-session players. This directly reinforces (rather than conflicts with) the earlier decision to include both local AND online 1v1 in the full MVP tier — the brief will document this as a dual-segment hypothesis, not a single locked persona.

## 2026-07-30 — Technical skills, content scope, differentiator

- **Engine/technical skills**: user explicitly does not have current game-coding expertise ("cela fait bien trop longtemps") and declined to state a preference rather than fake one. Decision: brief will document engine choice as **undecided/deferred to separate technical consultation**, not as a design decision — avoids inventing a false preference. Godot/Unity flagged conversationally as likely-natural 2D candidates for a solo dev but NOT adopted as a decision.
- **Roster size**: **8 characters** for the base roster (explicit Street Fighter 2 reference/homage in the choice itself).
- **Campaign structure**: sequential fights per character with weapon/bonus unlocks progressively earned along the way — explicit Soulcalibur comparison, consistent with earlier Genre Mashup findings.
- **Genuine differentiator (final, user's own words)**: even though the fantasy includes "bourriner," the player must wait for real openings — not firing blindly the instant the ball approaches, staying well-positioned relative to BOTH the ball AND enemy fire. This creates a **constant double-vigilance** (combat matchup awareness + ball-trajectory tracking, simultaneously, never pausable one for the other) that exists in neither Pong (spatial/anticipation only, no combat matchup) nor Street Fighter (matchup/reflex only, no third tracked object). This is the brief's positioning statement.
